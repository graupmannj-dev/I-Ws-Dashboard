Collector status and freshness

CAIRN prioritizes nontraditional operational, commercial and institutional signals. News indexes and conventional reporting primarily provide background and possible corroboration; neither fresh data nor multiple articles establishes a verified event. Collection dates are not event dates, and missing observations do not imply normal conditions.
