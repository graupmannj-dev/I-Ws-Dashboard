Coverage interpretation rules
An apparently quiet source is not proof that a hypothesis is false. Contradiction requires a specific falsifiable prediction, sufficient sensor coverage at the relevant place/time, and an explicit source-reviewed assessment. A document relay does not create a second independent observation.
