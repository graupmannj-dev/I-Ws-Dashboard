Discovery method · Why this is different

1 · Wide-area detection

Open-data changes in country pairs and conflict-event locations nominate candidates without requiring the region to exist on a predefined watchlist. Raw GDELT counts measure media-coded activity, not war probability.

2 · Source independence and costly behavior

Diplomatic, military, corporate, humanitarian, shipping, insurance, connectivity, and unofficial signals are grouped by their originating events. Derivative headlines do not count as independent confirmations.

3 · Dynamic regimes and silent divergence

Display absolute severity and novel change separately. Flag shifts in forecast, commercial, or civilian behavior while conventional signals remain flat. Record unknowns as not collected, never as negative indicators.
